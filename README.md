This repository serves as a **practical guide** for CRM leaders, Salesforce professionals, AI architects, and developers to learn how **AI, ML, and automation** can transform sales, service, and customer experience.

## 📖 Categories

### Marketing & Campaign Management
Use cases for campaign setup, segmentation, personalization, A/B testing, and automation.

### Lead Generation, Nurturing & Conversion
AI-driven methods for lead scoring, enrichment, qualification, and personalized follow-ups.

### Account & Opportunity Management
Intelligent account planning, opportunity forecasting, and product recommendations.

### Quote, Order & Subscription Management
Automating quote approvals, order creation, subscription renewals, and billing.

### Asset & Entitlement Management
Proactive asset lifecycle tracking, warranty monitoring, SLA management, and upgrades.

### Case & Support Management
AI-powered case routing, escalation rules, SLA monitoring, and knowledge base integration.

### Customer Experience & Retention
Churn prediction, onboarding personalization, upsell/cross-sell, and advocacy programs.

### Post-Support Analytics & Optimization
Measuring CSAT, support analytics, and predictive insights for process optimization.

### Customer Analytics & Sentiment Use Cases
Real-time sentiment analysis, frustration prediction, chat quality tracking, and experience insights.
